About SlimTide

Our Story

SlimTide was created for people who enjoy cooking simple, balanced meals at home. We believe that good food doesn't have to be complicated — a handful of fresh ingredients and clear instructions are often all you need.

What We Offer

SlimTide is a digital recipe collection organized by meal type. Every recipe is written in plain language with an ingredient list and step-by-step directions, so you can cook with confidence regardless of your experience in the kitchen.

Our Approach

We focus on everyday ingredients you can find at any local market and preparation methods that fit into a busy schedule. The goal is to make home cooking approachable and enjoyable.
