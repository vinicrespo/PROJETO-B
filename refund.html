Refund Policy

Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If SlimTide does not meet your expectations, you may request a refund within 30 days of your purchase date.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply contact us at support@slimtide.com with your order details. You may also request refunds directly through the platform where the purchase was made.

Processing

Once approved, refunds are processed to your original payment method. The time it takes to appear on your statement may vary depending on your payment provider.

Digital Product Notice

SlimTide is a digital product delivered electronically. Requesting a refund will revoke your access to the recipe collection.

Contact

For any questions regarding refunds, please contact us at support@slimtide.com.
